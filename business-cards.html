Before you print: Avery 5371 / 8371 / 8871 / 28877 (2″ × 3.5″, 10 per sheet). In the print dialog set paper to US Letter, scale to 100% (not “Fit to page”), margins to None, and turn Background graphics on — without it the ink colourway prints white. Run one plain-paper test and hold it against a blank Avery sheet before committing card stock. Turn cut lines off for the real run.
